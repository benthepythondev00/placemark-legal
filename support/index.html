Help and contact

Write to benthepythondev0@gmail.com. A person reads it, usually within two working days.

How do I cancel Placemark Pro?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Placemark › Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I was charged after the free week

The 7 free days turn into the yearly plan unless the trial is cancelled before it ends. Placemark sends a reminder two days before, if you allowed notifications. Apple handles all refunds: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose "Request a refund".

I paid, but Placemark is locked

On the paywall, tap Restore at the bottom. It finds purchases made with the Apple ID signed in on the phone.

How do I move my projects to a new phone?

Placemark saves a backup file every day you knit, in the Files app under On My iPhone › Placemark › Backups. In Settings, tap Export all to save it anywhere you like (iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email). On the new phone, open Settings › Restore and pick the file. Your PDFs are inside the backup.

My PDF won't open

Save the PDF to the Files app first, then add it from there. If it still doesn't open, write to us and say where the pattern came from (Ravelry, Etsy, a designer's site).
