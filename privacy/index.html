Placemark privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Placemark is a row counter for knitting and crochet patterns. It is built to keep your patterns on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your patterns. PDFs you import are copied into Placemark's own folder on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Your projects and counts. Rows, counters, ruler positions and settings are stored on your iPhone only.
  • Backups. Placemark saves a backup file to your iPhone's Files app (On My iPhone › Placemark) on days you knit. If you choose "Export all", you decide where that file goes.
  • Your onboarding answers (what you make, how you keep your place) are stored on your iPhone only.

We have no account system and no server that holds your data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple. To check whether you have an active subscription, Placemark uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created for this app, your purchase and renewal records from Apple, your device type and app version, and the country of your App Store account. It does not receive your patterns, projects or name. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple handles payment. We never see your card details.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, Placemark schedules one reminder on your phone two days before the trial ends. It is created on your iPhone; nothing is sent from a server.

Tracking

Placemark does not track you across apps or websites, shows no ads and does not sell data.

Children

Placemark is not directed at children under 13.
